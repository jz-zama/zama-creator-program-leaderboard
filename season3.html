Algorithm Transparency v0.2(click to expand)

We prioritize anti-gaming and quality. Final scores are computed per creator over the period, then prizes are allocated to the top 100 eligible accounts (≥5k followers) with a $30 CPM cap.

  1. Floors: Posts ≥ 1, Impressions ≥ 10k, Avg Post Impr ≥ 1k; ER > 5% → ban.
  2. Sanity: EngFixed = max(Engagements, Likes+RT+Quotes).
  3. Smart Follower → Impressions cap: < 10 → 30k; < 20 → 50k; < 50 → 100k; < 100 → 200k; < 200 → 350k; else no cap.
  4. Trust & Effective Impressions: EffImpr = Impr × max(0.45, min(1, Viral × Trust)), where Trust = clamp(SmartFollowerShare / 3%, 0.45..1).
  5. Propagation by reach: big‑reach baseline = max(0.20%, 0.30 × LikeRate). If (RT+Q)/Impr is below, plausibility is reduced.
  6. Micro‑viral corroboration (small accounts): If Followers < 5k and Impr ≥ 250k or FRR ≥ 40×, require PropRate ≥ 0.2%, RT/L ≥ 8%, Qshare ≥ 2%, SmartFollowerShare ≥ 1%.
  7. FRR tripwire: If FRR > 80× and PropRate < 0.08% → ban.
  8. Like‑rate caps by AvgReach: impossible > 5% (100k+), > 8% (20–100k), > 12% (5–20k), > 20% (2–5k), > 30% (<2k).
  9. Like‑dominant storms: At ≥ 20k AvgReach, if Likes dominate (>90%) and PropRate < 0.15% → ban.
  10. Mid‑reach balance (relaxed): If Impr ≥ 20k and Likes ≥ 300, ban only if Prop/Likes < 12% and Quotes/Likes < 0.5% and PropRate < 0.15%, unless strong absolute propagation (RT+Q ≥ 500 or RT ≥ 300 or Q ≥ 300).
  11. Quote‑dominant anomaly: If Impr ≥ 20k and Quotes ≥ 120 and RT ≤ 2 and low trust and PropRate < 0.5% → ban; also heavy soft penalty when quotes > 60% of (RT+Q) and RT/L < 5%.
  12. Score: Base = 2·log(1+EffImpr) + EffEng_cap + 0.2·√Likes, where EffEng_cap = min(Likes + 1.8·RT + 2.5·Quotes, EffImpr·ER_cap). Quality multipliers apply (smart‑share, RT/L, cadence, like‑rate, plausibility).
  13. Eligibility & prizes: Only ≥5k followers can be in visible Top‑100 and share $50k, split ∝ score^1.25 with $30 CPM cap and iterative redistribution.
